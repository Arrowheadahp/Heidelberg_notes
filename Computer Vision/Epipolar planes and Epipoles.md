### Epipole
This is the point on the image where the other camera can be seen
### Epipolar line
This is the line connecting the epipole to x (projection of a point in 3d). This line is the projection of the epipolar plane.
### Epiploar Planes and Epipoles

The epipolar line calculated from camera 1 of point $x_0$ as $$ l_1^Tx_1 = x_0^TFx_1$$
$$l_1^T=x_0F $$
The epipolar line calculated from camera 0 of point $x_1$ as $$ x_0^Tl_1^T = x_0^TFx_1 $$
$$l_0=Fx_1 $$
The epipole $e_0$ can be calculated as $$ e_0^TF = 0^T$$
