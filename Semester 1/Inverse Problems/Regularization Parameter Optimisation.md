We use Regularization techniques like [[Tikhonov Regularization]] for decreasing the Variance of the predicted value. But the choice of $\lambda$ can change the predictions a lot. Motivation is how to choose a good value of it. 
### [[L-Curve]]
The $\lambda$ essentially trades off between increasing regularization term from [[Data Term]]. This is the reason for using the [[L-Curve]] which is an empirical way of getting a good result of the parameter.
