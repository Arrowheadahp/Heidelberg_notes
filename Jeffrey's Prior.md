When we do not have any knowledge of the prior, we cannot assume the [[Uniform Distribution]] because:
1. [[Uniform Distribution]] cannot be normalized under infinite domain (since the probability becomes 0)
2. [[Uniform Distribution]] is not transform invariant. so we cannot assume that the $\lambda$ in Poisson is uniformly distributed 

Jeffery's Prior says that $$p(f)\propto\sqrt {I(f)}$$ where $I$= [[Fisher Information I]]
