From the [[Predicted Noise]], we get that $$f_{est}=A^{inv}g_{obs}=A^{inv}Af_{true}=RF_{true}$$Here R is the Model Resolution Matrix
- It describes how much $f_{true}$ contributes to $f_{est}$
- Diagonal R is perfect
R describes how well the inverse process uniquely identifies the solution.
- It typically is a blurred verson of the unit matrix.
- The smaller the blur, the better the final resolution
