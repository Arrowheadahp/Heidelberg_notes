Using the Conditional Probability rule in [[Probability Rules]], 
$$p(f|g)=\frac{p(g|f)p(f)}{p(g)}$$
where
- $p(f|g)$ is the posterior
- $p(g|f)$ is the likelihood
- $p(f)$ is the prior
- $p(g)$ is the evidence

To get the best value for $f$, we can 
1. maximize the likelihood: [[Maximum Likelihood]]
2. maximize the posterior: Maximum A posteriori
