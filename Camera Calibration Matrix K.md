The Final Calibration Matrix K is $$K=\begin{bmatrix}
f & sf & p_x+sp_y \\
0 & mf & mp_y \\
0 & 0 & 1
\end{bmatrix}$$
in the image coordinate system where the 
- $p_x,p_y$ are the centre of the image or the principal point of the image.
- $f$ is the focal point
- $m$ is the magnification factor in the $y$ direction
- $s$ is the skew because of non-rectangular matrix.

> [!NOTE] K has 5 Degrees of Freedom
### Orthogonal Projection
When the focal length is $f\to\infty$, then it is called orthogonal or parallel projection. Here FOV is the lowest.
